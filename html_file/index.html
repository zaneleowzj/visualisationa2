Exploring Australian Art

Discover how Australian artworks are distributed across states, art departments and time.

INTRODUCTION

Understanding Australia's Art Collection

Australian art reflects the country's diverse cultural identity, history and creative expression. This visualisation explores the distribution of artwork records across Australian states and territories, compares different art departments, and examines patterns within the available dataset.

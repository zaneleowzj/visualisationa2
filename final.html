A Journey Through Collection , Culture & Places

Exploring Australian Art

INTRODUCTION

Every artwork tells a story.

Through art collections, we can uncover patterns in geography, history, artistic forms and representation. Beginning with QAGOMA, this investigation explores what collection records reveal about Australian art — before turning our attention to the art that surrounds us in Melbourne.

Who tells its stories?

Examine creator backgrounds and artist representation within the collection.

About the data

The first part of this project examines records from the Queensland Art Gallery | Gallery of Modern Art (QAGOMA). Findings reflect the available dataset and should not be interpreted as a complete representation of all Australian art.
